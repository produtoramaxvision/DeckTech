# Support the project

DeckTech lets you control your computer's apps from your phone on your local
network. Supporting the project helps sustain maintenance, testing on devices,
documentation, and distribution. You do not need to pay to use its free features.

## Ways to support

### Voluntary contribution

Make a one-time voluntary contribution at [Support DeckTech](https://decktech.com.br/apoie).
You choose the amount and pay by card or Pix through Stripe. Do not use payment
details posted by other people in Issues or Discussions, and do not publish
payment receipts or personal data.

A voluntary contribution is not a DeckTech PRO purchase or a service contract.
It does not grant a license, a specific feature, or priority technical support.

### Sponsorship

Individuals and companies can propose one-time or recurring support. Describe
what you want to support and what you can contribute. See the partnership
options below; amounts and any benefits require a written agreement.
Write to [parcerias@decktech.com.br](mailto:parcerias@decktech.com.br).

### DeckTech PRO

DeckTech PRO is a separate paid product. Check availability and terms at
[decktech.com.br](https://decktech.com.br).
Do not send a donation as payment for a license.

### Feedback and sharing

- Report reproducible problems and suggest improvements using the
  [participation guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Help improve documentation and share useful experiences without sensitive data.
- Share [decktech.com.br](https://decktech.com.br) with people who can use it,
  respecting the rules of each community.

## Sponsorships, partnerships, and collaborations

We welcome proposals for:

- Financial support for maintaining and testing DeckTech.
- Documentation, translations, tutorials, and community collaborations.
- Commissioned projects, evaluated separately; talk to us.

Depending on the agreement, a sponsor can receive an acknowledgment with a name
or logo and a link in an agreed location, or a summary of the supported work.
These are options to discuss, not benefits included in a donation. Placement,
duration, permissions to use a brand, and any deliverables must be agreed in
writing before payment. Sponsorship does not guarantee audience, sales,
endorsement, an implementation date, or control over product decisions. It
does not provide access to the app's source code or users' data.

## Made by Produtora MaxVision

Produtora MaxVision is the studio behind DeckTech and is responsible for the
product, based in São Paulo/Brasil. DeckTech is proprietary software: the public
repository contains documentation, community channels, and releases, not the
app's source code.
Learn about the product at [decktech.com.br](https://decktech.com.br).

## Hire us

You can hire Produtora MaxVision for projects and services separate from
DeckTech. Send your brief to [parcerias@decktech.com.br](mailto:parcerias@decktech.com.br)
to discuss a commissioned project. Describe the problem, the expected
deliverables, constraints, budget range, and desired schedule. Availability,
feasibility, scope, price, and deadlines are confirmed in a separate proposal;
a conversation or a contribution does not book work.

## Contact

For contributions, sponsorships, partnerships, or a project brief, use the
email [parcerias@decktech.com.br](mailto:parcerias@decktech.com.br) and identify your subject. Do not post
confidential proposals in public threads. For installation and technical
questions, use [Support](SUPPORT.md); financial support is never required to ask
for help.
