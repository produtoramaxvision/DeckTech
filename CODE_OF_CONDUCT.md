# Code of Conduct

This space serves people who install, use, and participate in DeckTech Issues and Discussions.

Communicate clearly and respectfully. Criticism must address the product and ideas, never the person. Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of personal data, and abusive content are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close conversations, and block participants to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to [contato@decktech.com.br](mailto:contato@decktech.com.br) with the subject Conduct.
